1. Psychological Support
You can receive support and guidance from the following professionals:
Student Wellbeing Advisor (=Opintokuraattori)
Study Psychologist (=Opintopsykologi)
Study Coach (=Opintovalmentaja)
Special Education Teacher (=Laaja-alainen erityisopettaja)

How to book:
Appointment System: Book via Vihta system at https://vihta.metropolia.fi/
Direct Contact: Email firstname.lastname@metropolia.fi

3. Available Services:
Quiet Study Rooms: Can be booked via Outlook/Tuudo. Ideal for students needing focus.

General Info: https://www.metropolia.fi/fi/asiakastyot-ja-palvelut/kirjasto

5. Leisure & Campus Activities
Billiards: Located on the 7th floor. Sticks and balls are available for use from first floor.
Gaming Corner: iWall and board games are available on the 5th floor.
Student Clubs: Various clubs (gaming, sports, culture) are organized. Check the student portal for the latest club events.
