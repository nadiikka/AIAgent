# Metropolia Student Helpmate

Flask-based web application designed to support students who may struggle to find the right academic or wellbeing resources.
Many students know *what* they need help with, but not *where* to look.
The app recognizes when a student may be stressed, overwhelmed, or emotionally struggling, and adjusts its responses with gentle, supportive guidance.

Students often don’t know:
* where to find tutoring information
* how to access wellbeing services
* what resources exist at Metropolia
* where to start when overwhelmed by academic tasks

This app demonstrates how AI can improve accessibility, reduce stress, and guide students toward the right support—quickly and compassionately.
Problem understanding, and AI integration for Metropolia’s coding assignment. It highlights how a small, focused application can solve a real student-life challenge using modern AI tools.
